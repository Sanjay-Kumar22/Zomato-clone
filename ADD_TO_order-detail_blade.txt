In resources/views/customer/order-detail.blade.php, find a good spot in the order status
area (near the Live Tracking section, or near the top where order status/badge is shown)
and add this — it only shows once a delivery agent is assigned, and hides once the order
is delivered/cancelled:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
@if($order->delivery_agent_id && !in_array($order->status, ['delivered', 'cancelled']))
<a href="{{ route('customer.chat.show', $order) }}" class="btn btn-outline-danger btn-sm rounded-pill">
    <i class="bi bi-chat-dots-fill me-1"></i>Chat with Delivery Partner
</a>
@endif
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

That's the only entry point needed on the customer side.
