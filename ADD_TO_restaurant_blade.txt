In resources/views/customer/restaurant.blade.php, find the restaurant header area
(near where the "Favorite" heart button or restaurant name/badges are — search for
`rest-header` or the closing of the header block), and add this button anywhere
sensible in that header, e.g. right after the Favorite button:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
@auth
<form action="{{ route('customer.group-orders.start', $restaurant) }}" method="POST" class="d-inline">
    @csrf
    <button type="submit" class="btn btn-outline-danger btn-sm rounded-pill">
        <i class="bi bi-people-fill me-1"></i>Start Group Order
    </button>
</form>
@endauth
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

That's the only visual entry point needed — clicking it creates a new group order and
takes the host straight to the shareable group-order page.
